Privacy Policy

Appeal Evidence Bridge
Published by Safe Passage Strategies LLC
Last updated: April 16, 2026

Overview

Appeal Evidence Bridge is a browser extension that detects when a veteran's VA.gov appeal page indicates the record is open for new evidence, and injects a button linking directly to the VA's QuickSubmit electronic submission tool.

Data Collection

This extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data of any kind.

Specifically, the extension does not:

  • Collect personally identifiable information
  • Read, store, or transmit the content of VA.gov pages
  • Track browsing history or user activity
  • Use cookies or any local storage mechanism
  • Communicate with any external server
  • Share data with any third party

How the Extension Works

The extension reads the visible text of the current VA.gov page solely to detect whether specific phrases indicating an open evidence window are present. This detection occurs entirely within the user's own browser. No data leaves the user's device at any point.

Permissions

The extension requests the activeTab permission to read the text content of the currently active VA.gov tab. This permission is used exclusively to detect appeal status language and inject the QuickSubmit guidance banner. No other use is made of this permission.

Third Party Services

This extension links to the VA's QuickSubmit tool hosted at eauth.va.gov. Safe Passage Strategies LLC is not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. Users who click the QuickSubmit link are subject to the VA's own privacy policy and terms of service.
